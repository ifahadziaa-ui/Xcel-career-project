## 1. Brief

**What XcelCareers is.** A healthcare staffing and government contracting company. It places nurses and care staff with hospitals, clinics, long-term care, behavioral health providers, and county and state health departments. It supports local, state and federal government with staff augmentation, contract staffing, task orders and consulting. IT, finance and cybersecurity specialists sit across both. It is a minority veteran-owned small business, Maryland MBE/DBE/SBE certified.

**The website's job, in order**
1. **Win clients.** Show healthcare organizations, government buyers and prime contractors that XcelCareers is a certified, trusted partner, and make requesting staff easy.
2. **Prove it's contract-ready.** Show the certifications, CAGE, UEI, NAICS and past performance, with the capability statement as a download.
3. **Attract candidates.** Bring in healthcare, IT, finance and cyber professionals to apply.

**Audiences, by priority**
1. Employers and hiring managers.
2. Government contracting officers and prime contractors.
3. Healthcare job seekers.
4. IT and cyber job seekers.
5. Candidates already in the network.

**The one action per visitor**
- Employers, government buyers and primes: **submit a staffing request**, download the **capability statement**, or **book a short call**.
- Candidates: **apply** for an open role or **upload a résumé**.

**Voice: caring, trustworthy, professional.**
- Use plain words and short sentences, with real numbers in context.
- Never use cheesy stock photos or a casual tone, and never let the site look like a generic job board.
- Banned words: "solutions", "world-class", "cutting-edge", "passionate", "one-stop shop".

**Big idea: the people behind the care.** XcelCareers staffs the bedside and everything behind it: the nurse, the case manager, the billing team, the systems and the security. That reach across care, government and the specialists behind both is what no single-lane agency offers.
